# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The site owner and anyone they share it with for testing. There are no real customers.

## Product Purpose

**Northlight** is a fictional consumer-device brand used as a **test site**. It exists to test layout, responsiveness, animation and loading speed on a premium product-storefront structure. It replaced the Chennai Plots marketplace in this repository in November 2026 (that site remains in the Git history).

Success means the pages behave correctly at every screen size, the interactions work, and the pages load fast.

## Positioning

A test bed, not a business. Nothing is for sale and no real company is represented.

## Operating Context

- Two pages: `index.html` (store home) and `lumen.html` (a product page).
- Both pages are generated by `python3 scripts/build.py`. Edit the script, not the generated files.
- All CSS and JS are inline. The only external request is the Archivo font from the jsDelivr CDN (pinned commit), with a local fallback in `assets/fonts/`.
- Pages carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` because this is a test site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home: translucent sticky nav with a full-screen mobile menu, a demo-notice ribbon, three full-width product tiles, a two-column grid of four tiles, a scroll-snap carousel with previous/next buttons, a three-up services row and a directory footer that becomes accordions on small screens.
- Product page: sticky local nav, hero, two feature sections, a finish picker that recolours the phone illustration, a comparison table and a buy band.
- Motion: reveal on scroll, a scroll-linked zoom on device art where the browser supports it, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- Search, the bag and "Buy" are demos: they show a toast and never take an order or collect details.
- Devices are original inline SVG illustrations. There are no product photos.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Northlight. Mark: a four-point star. Fictional products: Lumen (phone), Arc (laptop), Slate (tablet), Pulse (watch), Halo (earbuds).
- Palette: near-black and mist grey surfaces, teal links, a coral "New" accent. Typeface: Archivo.
- The site must not use any real company's name, logo, product names, photos or copy.

## Evidence on Hand

None is real. Product names, prices, specifications and battery figures are invented placeholders, and the footer says so. Do not present any of it as fact, and keep the "fictional brand, test site" notice in the ribbon and footer.

## Product Principles

1. **Clearly a test.** The fictional status stays visible at the top and bottom of every page.
2. **Original, not copied.** Use common storefront patterns with Northlight's own identity and artwork.
3. **Nothing transacts.** No checkout, sign-in or data collection.
4. **Fast by default.** Inline code, drawn artwork, one font file.
