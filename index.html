What to check before you buy a plot

Three approvals come up on almost every Chennai plot. Here is what each one means.

DTCP approval

The Directorate of Town and Country Planning approves layouts outside the Chennai Metropolitan Area, which covers most plots along GST Road, OMR beyond the city and towards Chengalpattu.

CMDA approval

The Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority approves layouts inside the Chennai Metropolitan Area. A plot needs one or the other, depending on where it sits.

RERA registration

Larger projects must also be registered with the Tamil Nadu Real Estate Regulatory Authority. Ask the developer for the registration number and look it up on the TNRERA website.

  • Documents to ask for:
  • Sale deed
  • Approval copy and layout plan
  • Encumbrance certificate
  • Patta
  • Parent documents

Frequently asked questions

Chennai Plots is a listing marketplace. You buy from the developer, not from us.

Does Chennai Plots sell plots?

No. Chennai Plots lists plot layouts from developers. You visit, negotiate, book and register directly with the developer of the layout.

What does "Verified developer" mean?

The developer has confirmed the details shown on its listings with Chennai Plots. It is not a substitute for checking the approval copy and title documents yourself before you book.

Why are some listings marked "Sample listing"?

They are placeholders that show how listings from other developers will look. They are not real projects and cannot be booked.

Why is there no price on the listings?

Plot prices change with availability and plot size, so developers share current pricing when you enquire.

Do you list flats, villas or farmland?

No. Chennai Plots lists residential plot layouts only.
