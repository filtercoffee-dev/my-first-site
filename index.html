FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Call us on +91 72001 41515.

Are your projects DTCP and RERA approved?

Yes. Our projects are DTCP and RERA approved where applicable, and approval details are shared with every buyer before booking.

Can I get a bank loan for a plot?

Yes. Our approved layouts are eligible for loans from leading banks, and our team helps with the documentation.

Do you offer free site visits?

Yes. We arrange pick-up and drop for site visits on weekdays and weekends.

I live abroad. Can I still buy?

Yes. NRIs can view projects on live video tours, review documents online and complete the purchase with guidance on power of attorney.

What documents will I receive?

Sale deed, approval copies, encumbrance certificate, patta and other title documents as applicable to the project.
