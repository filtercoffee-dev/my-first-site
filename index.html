Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us on 72001 41515.

Are your projects DTCP and RERA approved?

Yes. Our plots, villas, apartments and farmlands are DTCP and RERA approved where applicable, and approval details are shared with every buyer before booking.

Can I get a bank loan for a plot?

Yes. Our approved layouts are eligible for loans from leading banks, and our team helps with the documentation.

Do you offer free site visits?

Absolutely. We arrange pick-up and drop for site visits on weekdays and weekends. Book a slot using the form on this page.

I live abroad. Can I still buy?

Yes. NRIs can explore projects through live video tours, review documents online and complete the purchase with guidance on power of attorney.

What documents will I receive?

You'll receive the sale deed, approval copies, encumbrance certificate, patta and other title documents as applicable to the project.
